🏛️ Institutional 9 Legendary Strategies IPO Screening Logic
Rules-Engine Evaluated
Warren Buffett:

Inherently skeptical of IPOs as promoters pick market peaks to sell. Requires an undeniable moat, high ROCE, and penalizes heavy Offer for Sale (OFS).

Charlie Munger:

Inversion checklist: searches for aggressive pro-forma adjustments, insider cashing-out, and governance litigation. Avoids corporate gamesmanship.

Benjamin Graham:

Demands a steep valuation discount to mature listed peers and tangible net asset backing. Avoids hype-driven multiples with zero margin of safety.

Peter Lynch:

Looks for easily understandable consumer or niche businesses capable of 10x geographic expansion at a reasonable PEG ratio ($< 1.2$).

Joel Greenblatt:

Applies the Magic Formula: top-quartile pre-IPO ROCE ($> 20\%$) combined with high post-money Earnings Yield ($EBIT / EV > 7\%$).

Rakesh Jhunjhunwala:

Big India growth story: high operating leverage, massive domestic addressable market, and backing visionary leadership in secular industries.
